# LP Research Intake

Use this skill when the user wants to add new LP research items to the design research pipeline before full analysis.

The expected outcome is a traceable intake record:
- A stable LP ID such as `LP-0214`, continuing from the current Notion/Dropbox maximum.
- PC and SP screenshots for first view and full/section map.
- Source and detail URLs preserved, including the actual captured page URL.
- Originals stored in Dropbox under the design research library.
- Lightweight Notion images generated for Free-plan-safe viewing.
- A temporary Notion page in `01｜LPリサーチライブラリー`, marked as not yet analyzed.
- When requested after analysis, corrected screenshots reflected back to Dropbox/Notion and validated through the repository checks.

For the detailed operating rules, read [references/lp-intake-workflow.md](references/lp-intake-workflow.md) before acting.

Do not treat the skill itself as permission to mutate external systems. Before creating, uploading, moving, deleting, or committing anything, use the available tools normally and respect the current user's request and approval requirements.
